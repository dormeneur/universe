**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forced a decision? What constraints were in play? Keep it factual — this is the part future-you will actually need.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly in one or two sentences.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it lost. This is usually the most valuable section: it stops the same option being re-proposed and re-rejected six months later.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to. Include the costs honestly — an ADR that only lists benefits isn't a record, it's advocacy.
